VOLLEYBALL, WITHOUT THE CLIPBOARD

VolleyScore

Keep the score, follow the momentum, and send the family a clean recap.

VolleyScore is an offline-first match companion built for parents, volunteers, and coaches. Start quickly, score every rally with one hand, undo mistakes, celebrate set wins, and save thoughtful match reports.

Parent Mode

Fast team setup, volleyball set scoring, swipe or button controls, clear undo, tactile feedback, celebrations, match photos, and shareable family recaps.

Advanced Coach Stats

Optionally tag rally outcomes without slowing down scoring. Review point sources, lost-point causes, serving and scoring runs, rotation differentials, trend graphs, coach notes, photos, and a polished post-match report.

Private by design

No login. No in-match ads. Match information stays on your device unless you explicitly choose to share it.
