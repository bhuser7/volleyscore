Quick answers

How do I score a rally?

Swipe left for your team and right for the opponent. If you prefer two large point buttons, turn on Button scoring during the match.

Can I undo a mistake?

Yes. Tap Undo to reverse the most recent scoring action. Timeout controls also allow an accidental timeout to be removed.

What is Coach Mode?

Enable Advanced coach stats during setup to optionally tag rally-ending outcomes such as kills, aces, blocks, service errors, attack errors, and opponent errors. A point is always recorded immediately; tagging is optional.

Where are completed matches stored?

Saved matches and coach notes remain on your device. Open Older Games from the setup screen to review reports, box scores, trends, and attached match photos.

How do camera and coach notes work?

Capture opens the system camera after you grant permission. Photos are saved to your Photo Library. The microphone button converts speech into a text coach note after you grant microphone and speech-recognition access.

Does VolleyScore require an account or internet connection?

No account is required. Core scoring, saved matches, and reports are designed to work offline. Sharing and Apple speech services may require connectivity depending on the service you select and your device settings.

How do I remove my saved information?

Delete individual matches from Older Games. Deleting VolleyScore removes its locally stored settings, matches, and notes. Photos already saved to your Photo Library can be deleted in Apple Photos.
