Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

Contact: hnfdevs@gmail.com

Summary

VolleyScore is an offline-first volleyball scoring and match-recap app. No account is required. hnfdevs does not use the app to track you, show advertisements, or sell personal information.

Information stored on your device

VolleyScore may store team names, preferences, match scores, rally outcomes, coach notes, reports, and match history locally on your device. This information is used only to provide app features.

Camera and photos

If you choose Capture, VolleyScore requests camera access so you can take a match photo. With your permission, captured photos are saved to your Apple Photo Library. Photos are not uploaded to hnfdevs. You control and delete those photos through Apple Photos.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you choose to dictate a coach note, VolleyScore requests microphone and speech-recognition access. Audio is used to create the text you requested. Speech recognition is provided through Apple system services and may use Apple servers depending on your device, language, connectivity, and Apple settings. hnfdevs does not receive or retain the recording.

Sharing

When you choose Share, iOS presents compatible apps and services. VolleyScore sends the report or recap only to the destination you select. The receiving service's privacy policy governs information you choose to share.

Location

VolleyScore does not request or collect precise location. Dates shown in saved matches come from the device clock.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

  • No third-party advertising SDKs
  • No cross-app tracking
  • No developer-operated analytics collection
  • No sale of personal information

Data retention and deletion

Delete a saved match from Older Games to remove that match and its locally managed report data. Deleting the app removes VolleyScore's locally stored settings, matches, and notes. Photos saved in your Photo Library must be deleted separately in Apple Photos.

Children

VolleyScore is suitable for family use but is not designed to collect personal information from children. No account or personal profile is required.

Changes to this policy

If VolleyScore's data practices change, this page will be updated before or alongside the applicable app release, with a revised effective date.
